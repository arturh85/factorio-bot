# `wall_ms` was removed, not fixed

## What it was actually measuring

`Event::wall_ms` (`crates/core/src/record/mod.rs`) was set in
`RunRecorder::record()` from `self.started.elapsed()` **at the moment
`record()` was called**, not at the moment the event it describes happened.

`record.actions()`, `record.teleports()` and `record.refusals()`
(`crates/scripting_lua/src/globals/record.rs`) are each called exactly once
per supervisor "ran" transition (`scripts/research_run.lua`) -- i.e. only
*after* an entire multi-bot plan has finished executing against the live
game, which can be minutes of real time and thousands of game ticks. Every
`ActionDispatched`/`ActionSettled`/`Teleport`/`PlacementRefused` event
written by one such call therefore got the wall-clock reading from the
moment that whole batch was flushed to disk, not the moment its underlying
game action actually happened. That is exactly the mechanism behind the
`33780 -> 738866` jump against a 10-tick move reported in
`docs/superpowers/notes/2026-09-02-inventory-shortfall.md`: the first event
of a new batch can have a tick barely past the previous batch's last one,
while its `wall_ms` reflects everything else in that new batch finishing
much later, in real time, before the batch was written.

(`MilestoneStarted`/`MilestoneSatisfied`/`MilestoneStuck`/`PlanCreated`/
`RunStarted`/`RunFinished` were not affected -- the Lua driving loop calls
those synchronously at the actual moment of the transition, never batched.)

## What it means now

The field is gone. It was traced to the exact call sites above and could not
be made to mean "real time this event happened" without adding new
capability, not fixing existing wiring: the true moment is only known inside
the executor's async dispatch loop (`ExecutionLog::start`/`observe`/
`start_walk`/`observe_walk` in `crates/executor/src/log.rs`, called from
`crates/executor/src/run.rs`), and getting it from there into
`record.actions()` would mean adding real-timestamp fields to the executor's
`Attempt`/`WalkObservation` types, threading them across the mlua boundary
(`build_observation` in `crates/scripting_lua/src/globals/goal/run.rs`), and
updating every existing test that asserts those types' shape -- a genuine
cross-crate feature addition, not a bug fix, and not something I could
validate end-to-end without running a live Factorio instance.

Given nothing consumes the field and a batch-stamped column that looks like
a per-event timestamp is worse than no column at all, it was removed rather
than left wrong or relabeled to a technically-honest-but-useless "batch
flush time" meaning.

Old runs already on disk stay readable: an `events.jsonl` line with a
leftover `"wall_ms"` key deserializes fine as an ordinary ignored extra
field (`Event`/`EventKind` have no `deny_unknown_fields`). No
`#[serde(default)]` was needed for this removal.

## What reads it

Nothing did, on either side:

- Rust: only test-fixture helpers (`crates/core/src/record/lanes.rs`,
  `crates/core/src/record/splits.rs`) constructed it, and only to satisfy
  the struct's shape.
- TypeScript: `app/src/lib/runTimeline.ts`, `runDiff.ts`, and the analysis
  views never read `.wall_ms`; it only appeared as a required key in
  `app/src/api/types.ts` and in test fixtures that had to include it to
  satisfy the type.

This made removal low-risk: there was no computation or display anywhere
that needed adjusting for its absence.

## Changes

- `crates/core/src/record/mod.rs` -- dropped `wall_ms` from `Event`, dropped
  the now-dead `RunRecorder.started: Instant` field, updated `record()`,
  added a test (`wall_ms_is_gone_but_an_old_line_carrying_it_still_reads`)
  pinning both backward-compatible reads of old lines and that fresh writes
  never re-add the key.
- `crates/core/src/record/lanes.rs`, `crates/core/src/record/splits.rs` --
  updated their `ev()` test helpers to match the new `Event` shape.
- `app/src/api/openapi.snapshot.json` -- regenerated via
  `UPDATE_OPENAPI_SNAPSHOT=1 cargo test -p factorio-bot-server --features lua
  --test openapi`.
- `app/src/api/types.ts`, `app/src/api/openapi.contract.spec.ts` -- dropped
  `wall_ms` from the `Event` type and its contract assertion (the seam that
  fails from both ends).
- `app/src/lib/runDiff.spec.ts` -- dropped `wall_ms: 0` from its `Event`
  fixtures.
- `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-01-run-recording-and-replay-design.md` --
  amended D1 to record the removal and why, without rewriting the historical
  decision.
